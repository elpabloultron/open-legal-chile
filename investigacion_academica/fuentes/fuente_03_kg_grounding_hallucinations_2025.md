## Resumen / Abstract
General-purpose LLMs exhibit an intrinsic Anglo-American Common Law bias, frequently injecting alien legal concepts (such as punitive damages, at-will employment, and grand jury indictments) into Continental Civil Law queries. This study proves that graph-grounded retrieval over formal civil codes resolves jurisdictional contamination.

## Conexión y Relevancia para Open Legal Chile
Valida el principio fundacional de Open Legal Chile: la prohibición estricta de extrapolar categorías de Common Law al derecho chileno, resolviendo la colonización doctrinal mediante las 9.863 instituciones interconectadas de LegalGraphify y el corpus BCN.
